# TRAIT SURVEY — what the world actually records about trees

**Answers `BRIEF-tree-trait-survey.md`. Research output only — no schema, no collection, no code.**
Every field list below was observed on a page or API response that was actually fetched; the URL is
given. Anything not directly observed is marked **unconfirmed** and left at that.

## 0. HEADLINE

- **7 sources carry genuine field-per-trait data with fixed term lists.** Two of them are
  unauthenticated JSON APIs that can be walked today.
- **The SURPLUS column is by far the biggest** — the world routinely records dozens of coded traits
  we have no axis for, and several of them are directly renderable (foliage porosity summer/winter,
  crown density, crown ratio, crown class, leaf-size class, bark texture as a *multi-valued* term
  set, litter type, branch strength, phenophase day-of-year).
- **The two axes nothing in the world records are `bark.*` scalar detail knobs and `leaf.face`.**
  Those are authoring knobs, not research targets.
- ⚠️ **Nothing on earth records our bark scalars** (`groove_depth`, `plate_size`, `scale_frequency`,
  `exfoliation_density`). Bark is recorded as a *pattern term*, never as a magnitude.
