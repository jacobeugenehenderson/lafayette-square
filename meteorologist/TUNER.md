---

## 1. The compositional stack

Four levels, bottom to top, plus one orthogonal pass:

1. **Primitives** — the shader instrument (noise modes, lighting). The *expandable* thing. Today: FBM + single-pass domain warp.
2. **Atoms** — individual visual characteristics, each generated by a primitive (flat dark base, fibrous streak, cauliflower lobe, cellular cells). This is what the existing **16-modifier enum** (`CANON.md §Modifier enum`) already gestures at — modifiers are "layered compositionally in the shader," i.e. atoms.
3. **Species** — a named stack of atoms (`cumulus_humilis` = a small program in the instrument). **Reusable procedural blocks, not finished pictures.** The roster + IDs are a contract (`CANON.md`) — sticky once the Almanac references them.
4. **Conditions** — the top, the "Simulation": select + layer *multiple* species (+ wind/rain/lightning) into a whole-sky look.

**TOD (time of day) is not a rung.** It is color + light applied *across* the whole stack — orthogonal. For getting *shape* right it's just the lighting context. (Consistent with `CANON.md`: "TOD lighting is NOT a preset axis.")

**The unification (the sign the shape is right):** "stacking atoms inside a species" and "layering species inside a Condition" are *the same operation* — one composable-layer mechanism serves both. Build it once, pays off twice.

## 2. The Tuner — the agentic fidelity loop

A two-designer conversation gated by an overseeing Critic. **The cast (4)** — full contracts in §8.3:

- **Designer I & II** — two symmetric makers; tune with the existing knobs, conversing **a quality at a time** with **comparative** checks ("wispier than before?"); grow their characteristics list; recognize when a new knob is needed.
- **Critic** — the overseer: holds the reference, says "Go," stays *out* of the work, then gates what the designers present — a fast **triage** (obvious junk → back) then the careful **intent-blind** honest look → approve to the human, send back, or (human-authorized) route to the Tinkerer. The only role that faces the human.
- **Tinkerer** — makes new knobs when the instrument can't yet express a quality (always human-authorized).

**Why the Critic is held out (resolved D3 — honesty + gap-detection):** the designers carry the edit history, so they're anchored on their own effort ("we turned the knob, so it *must* show"). The Critic is **intent-blind** — sees only the result + the references — so its look is honest. And the **disagreement between the designers' "this works" and the Critic's "doesn't read right" is the capability-gap signal** — the instrument is missing a knob, or the characteristics list a quality. Overfit-to-one-*photo* is handled by photo variety + judge-twice, not by hiding photos.

**The dossier (the durable artifact, per species):** varied reference photos · the WMO descriptor · the **characteristic rubric** (named, scored axes) · notes mapping each characteristic → knob(s). Much of this already exists: `public/clouds/photos/` (43 images + `SOURCES.json`, which already flags `needs_photo` gaps), each preset's prose `description` + `tags`, and the knob→morphology notes in `CANON.md §Parameter conventions` (`warpAmp` = cauliflower-vs-smooth; `octaves` = structured-vs-sheet). The Tuner *formalizes* these into scored axes — it isn't gathering from zero.

**Rubric discipline:** decompose to **atomic, orthogonal characteristics** (a basis). Completeness = every atom has a knob that generates it. Orthogonality = each atom dial-able without disturbing the others (so "stacked qualities" are independently reachable). Holistic "looks the same" vibes drift run-to-run; per-axis scores don't.

**Three outputs, not one:**
1. Knob settings (when the instrument suffices).
2. **A capability gap → knob spec** (when it doesn't): the designers hit a quality they can't reach, the Critic's honest look confirms it's not there, and that becomes a build ticket for the Tinkerer. *This is the engine of §3.*
3. The growing rubric/vocabulary.

**Judge twice:** a species must be faithful **in isolation** (vs. its reference) **and** valid **under composition** (placed in a Condition alongside other decks). Over-fit to one photo and it looks wrong the moment a Condition stacks a veil over it.

**Loop hygiene:** keep best-so-far (never regress), lap to plateau (§8.4), give the Critic a *fixed*, reference-only standard so "approved" is stable. Watch designer↔designer self-anchoring — the intent-blind Critic is the mitigation.

## 3. Growing the instrument — no hard ceiling

When the loop can't match a cloud, the output is **a spec for the missing knob**, not "close enough." The instrument grows until every cloud is generable. The bake runtime (§4) is what makes this affordable — expensive primitives run at rebake-rate, not frame-rate — so fidelity is *not* bounded by the live frame budget.

**The wispy clouds need a fractal *family*, not "more FBM."** FBM (summed octaves) gives puffy/billow — good for cumulus, useless for cirrus, because isotropic noise has no direction and wisps are all direction. The cirrus cluster needs:

- **Ridged / inverted multifractal** (`1 − abs(noise)`) → blobs become **sharp filaments/veins**. Biggest single lever for "fibrous." (FBM = smooth puffs · `abs(noise)` = cauliflower billow · ridged = fibers — three composable primitives.)
- **Anisotropic stretch** → elongate structure along a shear axis → parallel drawn-out fibers (fibratus).
- **Curl / flow advection** → push density along a curl field → the swept, hooked mares'-tails (uncinus, fallstreaks). (Iterated domain-warp — "fbm of fbm" — is the canonical reference.)
- plus **translucency** — cirrus also reads as cirrus because it's thin/high/translucent; that's lighting + altitude (`baseAlt`, `thickness`, `edgeSilver`), not noise. Form + translucency *stacked*.

**Cluster atoms into shared primitives so it isn't 50 bespoke shaders.** One primitive serves a cluster: **cellular/Voronoi** → mackerel (Cc stratiformis) + honeycomb lacunosus + Sc lumps + lenticular cells; **anisotropic stretch + flow** → the whole cirrus family; **lobe/pouch displacement** → mammatus. Build the high-leverage shared primitives, mapped onto the existing modifier enum where one exists.

## 4. The bake-and-tween runtime

**The cost model, not just a perf trick.** Bake-when-updated runs the expensive procedural work **once per rebake**, not every frame — which is what removes the frame-budget ceiling from §3.

- **Bake the shape substrate** (cloud density/structure — the slow, expensive thing). **Not** final lit pixels.
- **Keep live:** TOD color/light (continuous all day but *cheap* — lighting over existing shape) and slow drift/advection.
- This is *why* "deep summer = one bake all day" works: shape is static even as the sun sweeps over it. Cadence tracks only the **shape's** rate of change. Storm churns → ~4-min rebakes; calm holds → one bake.

**Volatility is a Condition property.** A storm Condition declares high churn (~4 min); a stable one declares "hold." Plus an event trigger: rebake immediately when the active Condition *changes* (a front rolls in).

**Tween between bakes — where the modularity concentrates.** At any instant the live engine holds **two keyframe bakes + a blend `t`**, lerping, relit and drifted live on top. That one `lerp(A,B,t)` serves three jobs: intra-Condition evolution (keyframes minutes apart), inter-Condition transitions (clear→storm over 20 min), and the rebake hand-off cross-fade. It also **collapses the runtime to one code path** regardless of weather — calm = identical keyframes, storm = differing keyframes + advancing `t`; volatility only sets keyframe spacing and `t` speed, never *what runs*.

- **Why tween demands "bake shape, not pixels":** lerping two density fields *morphs* (A dissolves plausibly into B); lerping two lit images *ghosts* (double-exposure). So "bake shape, relight live" pays off twice — relightable **and** tweenable.
- **Division of labor:** tween handles *form-change*, live drift handles *position* — so the sky keeps moving even *between* keyframes; never a frozen frame.
- **Producer/consumer seam:** the baker runs async, off the render thread, publishing keyframes into a small ring buffer; the renderer samples the two most recent and tweens. They never block each other — a slow rebake just means the consumer keeps tweening toward the last good keyframe; no hitch. The entire expensive world hides behind **two textures + a float.**

This is a **freeze-the-substrate strategy** the app uses broadly — cartograph's prebake, the Arborist's baked artifacts — not a borrow from any one of them: freeze the expensive-derived substrate behind a clean interface, let a cheap consumer read it, refresh on change. Applied here to the sky. The Meteorologist stays a fully separable module; this is shared *doctrine*, not a dependency.

### 4.1 The bake domain is bounded by the Hero keyframes (Jacob, 2026-06-08 — resolves the target)

Sky is only needed for the LS **Hero shot**, and the Hero shot is driven by **camera keyframes**. So the system can compute *exactly* the potential visible area of sky = the **union of the Hero camera frustums**. This collapses the bake target from open-ended (whole dome / 3D volume) to a small, fully **enumerable** swath. Consequences:

- **Beautiful AND lightweight stop fighting.** Tiny known domain → pour everything into per-bake quality (max steps, multiple scattering, self-shadow), store almost nothing.
- **Representation = view-space along the Hero path,** not a world volume. Bake the high-quality cloud at each camera keyframe (× each weather sample) as a **relightable impostor** (density/depth/normal over the visible window — *not* lit pixels, so TOD still relights live). Parallax to cover = exactly the camera motion between keyframes (computable): barely-moving Hero → one impostor; sweeping Hero → a few baked views along the sweep, no more.
- **Two keyframe systems multiply into one small grid:** *(camera keyframe) × (weather sample)* — both bounded, both authored. Same `lerp` tweens across camera keyframes for the move and weather keyframes for the evolution.
- **Composition (D2) moves to bake-time.** The expensive march composes the decks (cumulus + cirrus…) once into the single visible impostor; the live runtime never layers — it samples one composed result. Multi-deck stops being a live-shader problem.
- **Drift becomes a within-view scroll** (not true volumetric advection) — fine and beautiful for a bounded view.
- **Dependency:** the sky bake hangs off the Hero camera keyframe rig; rebakes when keyframes or the Condition change. Both authored, both rare. Tidy.

This is the resolution of D1's target question (§7). It does *not* by itself decide D1's convention question (does adopting a baked impostor revisit "Math, not bakes"? — yes, but the swath is so small the texture-memory objection nearly vanishes).

## 5. The capture primitive — the gate on everything

The Tuner can't run until an agent can **set knobs → render → capture a PNG of the live export path** (the same pixels the slab ships, not a proxy — proxies have repeatedly misled in this project). It does not exist yet (`CanaryScene` is the viewport; nothing grabs a frame). Build this first. It is also what the §4.1 bake step captures, so the loop tunes exactly what ships *and* the tooling isn't throwaway.

**Scope (verified against the shader, 2026-06-09):**

- **Determinism is cheap — the key finding.** `atmosphere-materials.js` has **no TAA, no cross-frame accumulation, no reprojection** (its `accum` is *within-frame* ray compositing, not frame history). The only time dependence is `uTime` (wind advection, L148–156/250) + `uLightningFlash`. So each frame is a **pure function of (≈13 knobs + camera + `noiseSeed`)** once you pin `uTime`, wind, flash, and the TOD minute. Single frame, reproducible, no settle loop.
1. **Freeze-time capture mode** — pin `uTime`/wind/`uLightningFlash`/TOD-minute. (`noiseSeed` already a knob → seeded field.)
2. **Reuse the in-repo capture pattern, don't invent** — the Arborist sibling already grabs the canvas for specimen thumbnails (`src/arborist/SpecimenViewport.jsx:163`, `gl.domElement`; same in `src/preview/PreviewApp.jsx:120`). Needs `preserveDrawingBuffer: true` on the canary `<Canvas>` (or a synchronous read in the post-render `useFrame`).
3. **In-app hook** — `window.__tuner = { setKnobs, setContext({camera,tod}), freeze(), capture()→dataURL }`, driving `useMeteorologistStore`.
4. **Persistent headless session** — Playwright holds `meteorologist.html` open *once*, calls the hook hundreds of times (cold-launch per capture would dominate wall-clock).
5. **Camera presets** — reuse `canaryCamera.js` (isolation cam per-species; Hero cam for composed shots).
6. **Output** — `pngPath` + provenance `{ knobSet, camera, seed, tod }`.
- **⚠️ Risk:** headless Chromium without a GPU → SwiftShader software WebGL (slow raymarch, *possibly* pixel-divergent). Run the session GPU-backed, or verify SwiftShader is consistent early. The one real unknown.

## 6. Reconcile with canon (read before acting)

- **"Math, not bakes" (`README.md §Convention reminders`) — NARROWED 2026-06-08.** The original cloud convention was *no texture memory, procedural raymarch live*. §4 revisits it: a baked swath **is** texture memory. Resolved (D1): the rule now reads **"Math for the library; bake only the Hero swath"** — the authoring library stays procedural; the runtime may cache the keyframe-bounded Hero swath as a relightable impostor. The keyframe bound (§4.1) shrinks the texture-memory cost to near nothing, which is what justified the narrowing. ("No bake *ceremony*" — the authoring drafts/published split — is untouched; saves still write `*.json` directly.)
- **Multi-preset blend is param-averaging today** (`bindUniformsFromDirective`, `src/components/Atmosphere.jsx`; NOTES:193) — a weighted blend of uniform *values* across `clouds[]` into one slab. Composition (§1 level 4) and the bake/tween runtime (§4) both need **multi-deck layering** instead. This is the concrete architectural delta to land.
- **Sky-pivot ADR precedent** (`NOTES.md`, 2026-05-20/21): "procedural canon + sparse per-cell overrides," with the procedural function *sampled and committed as static hex data* per season. The bake-keyframe idea rhymes with it — procedural source-of-truth, materialized substrate, cheap live consumer. Worth mirroring its shape (sparse operator overrides on top of a procedural base) rather than inventing a parallel one.
- **Roster + IDs are a contract** (`CANON.md`). The Tuner moves *numbers*, freely; it does not churn the roster. New morphologies arrive as primitives/modifiers, not as preset proliferation (matches the modifier doctrine).
- **Weather nomenclature + model** (`WEATHER-MODEL.md`, settled 2026-06-08, concurrent window). That doc owns *what weather is* (Conditions = Condition × Degrees; continuous response; the live-service→emulator→slab pipeline); this doc owns *how the clouds get faithful + how the runtime affords it*. Vocabulary is shared (a "Condition" is the same thing in both). This doc's §4 two-clocks (shape-bake vs live-relight) **nest under** WEATHER-MODEL §5's two-clocks (weather-poll vs computed) — a Condition/Degree change drives a shape rebake. The runtime's directive (`useAtmosphere`) carries the per-Condition magnitudes (degree-driven via `src/lib/condition-degrees.js`); the baker consumes that directive.

## 7. Sequencing + open decisions

**Build order:**
1. **Capture primitive** (§5) — gates everything.
2. **Formalize one rubric** from existing `description`/`tags`/`CANON` conventions.
3. **`cumulus_humilis` loop end-to-end** — a cloud the current knobs can already hit; validates the *machinery* (failure here = a loop bug, not a shader ceiling).
4. **`cirrocumulus_stratiformis` (mackerel) — the gap cycle** — exercises the real product: loop fails → diagnoses "needs cellular primitive" → emits knob spec → build primitive → re-loop. *This cycle is the point.*
5. **Top 10**, decomposing into the atom basis along the way so the primitives built are the high-leverage shared ones.

**Open decisions for Jacob:**
- **(D1) Bake-vs-math — RESOLVED (2026-06-08): adopt.** Target = bake the Hero-keyframe-visible swath, view-space relightable impostor (§4.1). The convention is narrowed accordingly — `README.md §Convention reminders` now reads "Math for the library; bake only the Hero swath." Rationale: the keyframe-bounded swath shrinks texture-memory to near nothing, it unbounds fidelity (§3), and it mirrors the app-wide freeze-the-substrate doctrine.
- **(D2) Multi-deck layering** — *now a bake-time concern, not a live-shader one* (§4.1): the offline march composes decks into the single visible impostor. Confirm composition moves to bake-time rather than a live slab→decks change.
- **(D3) Agent topology — RESOLVED (2026-06-08): keep all three roles.** Generator + critic optimize in rubric space; the oracle judges holistically *without* the rubric. The gap between "rubric satisfied" and "reads as the species" is the capability-gap signal (collapsing them blinds the loop to it). Oracle = gate at convergence; overfit handled by photo variety + judge-twice. See §2.
- **(D4) First species — RESOLVED (2026-06-08): `cumulus_humilis`.** Maximal machinery shakedown — `warpAmp` cauliflower lobing (the dominant morphology lever, already in the shader) can almost certainly hit it, so a failure isolates a loop bug, not a shader ceiling; discrete puffs = unambiguous scoring; reference photo verified `ok`; common Condition base (composition-testable). `cirrocumulus_stratiformis` remains the first deliberate *gap* cycle (build-order #4).
- **(D5) Dome feel — curvature on the visible swath** (Jacob, 2026-06-08): make the keyframe-visible sky read as a curved dome, not a flat lid. **World-dome, NOT screen-lens** — model the clouds on a hemisphere shell in world space so the Hero camera sees real curvature through its normal lens (sky arcs overhead, foreground stays perspective-correct). *Avoid* a screen-space fisheye: it bends the whole frame (buildings, horizon) and reads as a camera gimmick. *Recommend:* bake the swath under the world-dome projection (curvature baked in → free at runtime, coherent across tweens); expose a **curvature-amount knob** as an authoring constant; optionally a *small* live screen-space radial trim for taste. Default subtle. Bonus: the dome's horizon compression adds depth, making the bounded swath feel like a much bigger sky.

## 8. The agent quartet — role contracts (build spec for `.claude/agents/` + the Workflow)

This is the operational spec for §2's loop. It is the **build target**, not yet built — two reframes govern it (from the 2026-06-09 advise): (a) the roles need the **shared tool surface (§8.1) before their prompts mean anything** — write the agent defs *against* a real interface, not on paper; (b) the durable home is **`.claude/agents/cloud-*.md` definitions (the roles) + one Workflow script (the loop)** — NOT 50 hand-written HANDOFF briefs (this project's one-shot-brief pattern is wrong for a loop that runs once per species). Refine the prompts empirically against the `cumulus_humilis` shakedown; don't perfect them in prose.

### 8.1 Shared tool surface (the GATE — build first)

Every agent is blind/handless until these exist. Each is a contract, not yet an implementation:

| Tool | Input → Output | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **`set-knobs`** | `KnobSet` → applies to the active preset | drives `useMeteorologistStore.setCloudParam`; the agent's *hands* |
| **`capture-render`** | `{ camera, tod, condition? }` → `pngPath` | grabs the **live export path** (not a proxy — §5); the agent's *eyes*. The single hardest dependency. |
| **`read-dossier`** | `speciesId` → `Dossier` | reference photos + WMO descriptor + rubric |
| **`compare-images`** | `{ render, reference[] }` → multimodal read | the visual-similarity judgment; used by the **Designers** (comparative, quality-by-quality) and the **Critic** (triage + holistic, intent-blind) |
| **`edit-shader` / `build`** | diff → build result + capture | **Solutions Architect only**; worktree-isolated |

### 8.2 Shared data contracts (objects passed between roles)

- **`Dossier`** = `{ speciesId, label, wmo, references: pngPath[], descriptor, rubric: Atom[] }`
- **`Atom`** (one rubric axis) = `{ id, name, description, primitive, knobs: knobKey[], targetStrength, weight }`
- **`KnobSet`** = `{ [knobKey]: number }` over the ~13 `cloudParamFields.js` params
- **`DesignerCheck`** (the comparative exchange between the two Designers) = `{ quality, achievedIntent: bool, direction, note }` — **comparative, not absolute**: "did the last edit move this quality the intended direction (vs. the prior frame / reference)?" not "rate it 0–1." A quality both designers tried for but **can't achieve** with current knobs becomes a `CapabilityGap` (surfaced from the dialogue).
- **`CriticVerdict`** = `{ triage: 'reject'|'pass', verdict?: 'pass'|'back'|'tinker', reason, missingCharacteristics? }`
- **`CapabilityGap`** = `{ speciesId, missingQuality, description, proposedPrimitive?, proposedKnobs?, references }`
- **`TinkererResult`** = `{ newKnobs: knobKey[], diff, buildOk, before: pngPath, after: pngPath }`

### 8.3 The roles

The quartet = **Critic + Designer I + Designer II + Tinkerer** (Designer is one role, two symmetric instances). Translate each into a `.claude/agents/cloud-<role>.md` (frontmatter `tools:` = its tools; system prompt = its Method + Guardrail).

**Critic — the overseer / reference-keeper / doorman.** Holds the ground truth, bookends the work, and is the only role that reaches the human. Judges at **two levels of scrutiny.**
- Inputs: `Dossier` (reference photos + data) + a presented `render`. Tools: `read-dossier`, `compare-images`. Dispatch: **context-isolated** — sees references + the result, **never the knob log or what was tried.**
- Method:
  - **Open:** holds up the pic + data, sets the target, says **"Go,"** then stays *out* of the designers' back-and-forth.
  - **Close — gate 1, Triage (fast, cheap):** the "don't waste my time" pass. *Obviously* not ready → straight **back to the designers**, no careful review, no human. (Automatable as a cheap-model pass.)
  - **Close — gate 2, Honest look (careful, intent-blind):** survivors get the considered *"does this read as the species?"* → **three moves:** **pass muster → show the human** (approve → write preset); **not there → back to the designers** (naming what's missing); **needs a new knob → show the human FIRST, then hand to the Tinkerer** (engaging the Tinkerer changes the shared instrument → human authorizes the spend before it builds).
- Output: `CriticVerdict` = `{ triage: 'reject'|'pass', verdict?: 'pass'|'back'|'tinker', reason, missingCharacteristics? }`. Guardrails: the honest look is **intent-blind** — never receives the edit history, so it can't fall for pride-of-ownership ("we turned the knob, so it *must* be visible"); judges against **the photos, not the designers' characteristics list.** Never turns a knob.

**Designer I & Designer II — the two makers (symmetric peers).** Make the cloud with the existing knobs; converse a quality at a time; know how to ask for new knobs.
- Inputs: `Dossier`, current `KnobSet`. Tools: `set-knobs`, `capture-render`, `read-dossier`, `compare-images`. Both symmetric — each can edit, capture, and judge.
- Method: one proposes a directional change on a single quality ("needs wispier") → edits knobs → captures → asks the partner the **comparative** question ("wispier than before?") → *yes* (next quality) / *no* (try again). Swap roles freely; **compile + grow their characteristics list** (the rubric) as they go. They **recognize** one of two states and present to the Critic: **"this works"** (a candidate) or **"we're stuck — this needs a new knob"** (a `CapabilityGap`). They flag the need; the **Critic** routes the knob request (through the human) to the Tinkerer.
- Output: a candidate `{ render, knobSet }` or a `CapabilityGap` — both to the Critic. Guardrail: only move knobs; never edit the shader; **never face the human** — everything routes through the Critic.

**Tinkerer — the knob-maker.** Engaged when a quality can't be reached with the current knobs — **after the human authorizes** (routed by the Critic).
- Inputs: a `CapabilityGap`. Tools: `edit-shader`, `build`, `capture-render`. Dispatch: **worktree isolation**, human-reviewed.
- Method: design + forge the new primitive/knob in `atmosphere-materials.js` + register it in `cloudParamFields.js`; prove with before/after captures; hand the new knob back to the designers.
- Output: `TinkererResult` = `{ newKnobs, diff, buildOk, before, after }`. Guardrail: writes the live shader (GPU/perf stakes) → **gated, never auto-merged**; prefer a shared primitive serving a cluster over a bespoke knob.

*(Human = **sees only decisions, never the thrash** — (1) a finished cloud to approve, or (2) a Tinkerer request to authorize. Everything in between stays hidden.)*

### 8.4 Orchestration — the human runs it first, automate later

**v1 is human-orchestrated** (Jacob, 2026-06-09): in practice the human is the router — pasting each role's brief, telling everyone where to go and when, sequencing the laps. So the table below isn't a script yet; it's the **human's run-sheet** — the decision logic executed by hand. That's the right first phase: running it manually *is* the shakedown that reveals what the briefs + routing actually need before any of it is hardened. (It mirrors how this project already dispatches specialists — by pasting briefs.) The circle laps **as many times as necessary**; no max-N.

| Trigger | → next |
|---|---|
| Designers: quality not right yet | keep conversing (edit → "better?" → yes/no) |
| Designers: "this works" | **Critic — triage** (fast) |
| Designers: "stuck — need a knob" | flag to the **Critic** (which routes it) |
| Critic triage: obviously not ready | straight **back to Designers** (don't waste time / the human) |
| Critic triage: plausible | **Critic — honest look** (careful, intent-blind) |
| Critic: pass muster | **Human** approves → write preset |
| Critic: not there | back to the **Designers** (what's missing) |
| Critic: needs a knob | **Human authorizes FIRST** → **Tinkerer** builds → new knob → resume |
| Tinkerer done | resume with the new knob |

**The human sees only decisions, and there are exactly two:** (1) approve a finished cloud, (2) authorize the Tinkerer. Everything between is the designers' thrash, hidden.

**v2 automates below the decision line, never above it.** Once the briefs + routing are proven by hand, harden the **high-frequency inner loop** (the designer↔designer comparative back-and-forth — cheap, repetitive) into a Workflow script. The **two human gates stay human permanently** — approve / authorize-Tinkerer are deliberate checkpoints, not scaffolding. So "human sees only decisions" is also the **automation boundary**: automate the thrash, keep the human at the gates. **Engaging the Tinkerer is human-authorized in every phase — an invariant.**

**Termination = plateau, not a cap.** Lap while the cloud keeps improving; declare **best-effort** when it plateaus **and** the designers have no knob left to ask for. A token/cost ceiling is a safety backstop only.

**Why laps compound:** the state carried between laps = best-so-far `KnobSet` + the designers' grown characteristics list + the grown instrument (new knobs). Each lap starts richer — productive instead of spinning.

**Why unbounded looping is safe (the escalation ladder):** every cycle must *either improve or escalate to a different lever* — re-tune → (human-authorized) new knob → add a characteristic → fresh designer strategy → fresh Critic look → human. Best-effort = the plateau where every lever is spent.

### 8.5 v1 deliverables — build order, briefs, run-sheet

**Build order:**
1. `set-knobs` + `capture-render` (§8.1, §5) — the gate; nothing runs without it.
2. `cumulus_humilis` `Dossier` (§8.2) — reference photos + descriptor + a starting characteristics list, from `public/clouds/photos/` + the preset's `description`/`tags` + CANON conventions.
3. The three briefs below — pasted by hand (v1); they become `.claude/agents/cloud-*.md` (frontmatter `tools:` + body) when automated.
4. Run `cumulus_humilis` by hand off the run-sheet; refine the briefs empirically. Only then scale to the top 10, and only then harden the inner loop into a Workflow (§8.4 v2).

> ⚠️ The briefs assume the §8.1 tool surface is live. Until then they're templates.

**The briefs** (paste-ready; Designer is one brief, instantiated twice):

**— Designer (I & II) —**
> You are a cloud **Designer**, one of two working as symmetric peers. Goal: make the rendered cloud match the reference **one quality at a time**, using only the existing knobs.
> **Receive:** the `Dossier` (reference photos + descriptor + data), the current `KnobSet`, your partner's last reply. **Tools:** `set-knobs`, `capture-render`, `read-dossier`, `compare-images`.
> **Method:** pick ONE quality ("needs wispier") → edit knobs toward it → capture → ask your partner a **comparative** question ("wispier than before?") → *no* = try again, *yes* = next quality. Keep a running characteristics list. When you both agree it's good → present `{render, knobSet}` to the **Critic**. When you both agree a quality **can't be reached** with current knobs → flag a `CapabilityGap` to the **Critic**.
> **Never:** edit the shader; face the human; use absolute 0–1 scores (judge comparatively).

**— Critic —**
> You are the **Critic**, the overseer. You hold the ground truth and are the only role that reaches the human.
> **Receive (open):** the `Dossier` — set the target, say **"Go."** **Receive (close):** a candidate render. You are **never** given the knob log or edit history. **Tools:** `read-dossier`, `compare-images`.
> **Method:** **(1) Triage** — *obviously* not ready? → reject, straight back to the designers (don't waste time). **(2) Honest look** (survivors only) — does it read as the species, judged against the **photos** (not the designers' list)? → **pass muster** (→ human approves), **not there** (→ back, name what's missing), or **needs a knob** (→ show the human FIRST, then route to the **Tinkerer**).
> **Never:** turn a knob; accept the edit history; let the human see anything that hasn't passed muster (besides a Tinkerer authorization).

**— Tinkerer —**
> You are the **Tinkerer**, the knob-maker. Engaged **only after the human authorizes**, when a quality can't be expressed with current knobs.
> **Receive:** a `CapabilityGap`. **Tools:** `edit-shader`, `build`, `capture-render` — in a **worktree**.
> **Method:** design + forge the new primitive/knob in `atmosphere-materials.js`, register it in `cloudParamFields.js`, prove with before/after captures, hand the knob back to the designers. **Prefer a shared primitive serving a cluster** over a bespoke one-off.
> **Output:** a PR-grade `TinkererResult` diff. **Never auto-merge** — human-reviewed.

**The run-sheet** (v1 — you are the router; you act at exactly two points):
1. **Critic** ← paste the `Dossier`. It sets the target + says "Go."
2. **Designers I & II** ← paste `Dossier` + "Go." They converse, tuning quality by quality (relay between the two, or run them in one shared thread).
3. They present a candidate → paste to the **Critic** (triage, then honest look):
   - *back* → return to the designers (2) with what's missing.
   - *pass* → **YOU approve or reject**; on approve, write the preset. ✅
   - *needs a knob* → **YOU authorize or not**; if yes, paste the `CapabilityGap` to the **Tinkerer** (4).
4. **Tinkerer** (worktree) builds → returns a diff → **you review/merge** → hand the new knob to the designers → back to (2).
5. Repeat to best-effort (plateau, §8.4). **Your only two actions: approve a cloud · authorize a knob.**
