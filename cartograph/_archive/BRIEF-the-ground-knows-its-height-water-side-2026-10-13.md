# The water-side half as it was planned (Diary, 2026-10-13)

Retired by Boz's ruling (2026-10-13), on Tern's measurement `scratch/coast/water-side.mjs` (f2c65925): "lower the water
side to LOW" is retired as an action. Jacob ruled the gusset IS "the bank from [high] down to the LOW-WATER plane"; he
did not rule that anything is lowered. "Lower, then S-curve" was the State block's way of carrying that out while the
coast was OSM's and the water side was a flat at y = 0. Once the coast is the lidar at high water, the water side is the
surveyed lidar flats and the bed, and lowering it would overwrite measured data.
