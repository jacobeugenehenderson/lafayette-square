    ⛔⛔ **THE 'LAMPS vs TREES DIVERGENCE' RECORDED HERE EARLIER WAS MY MISREADING AND IS EVICTED.** I read `bake-trees.js`'s *"acquire an inventory, or accept a uniform canopy"* as a ruling about **whether trees appear**.     ⭐⭐ **TREES ALREADY DO EXACTLY WHAT JACOB DESCRIBED, AND LAMPS SHOULD COPY THE PATTERN NEXT DOOR RATHER THAN INVENT ONE.** Measured on huron 2026-09-21: `clean/osm_trees.json` = **168 surveyed**, `clean/derived_trees.json` = **21,505 invented** (NLCD canopy fill) → 18,616 placed. The machinery that makes that honest is already built and is the spec for lamps:
    - **Two wells, and provenance is stamped per item** — `SOURCE_BY_BASENAME` (`bake-trees.js`) maps each well to a `source`: `osm` for real positions, `derived` for the invented fill. ⇒ Lamps want `osm_lamps.json` + `derived_lamps.json` in the same shape.
    - **Cross-well dedup keeps the RICHEST record** — a real inventory beats OSM's real-but-sampled position, which beats the fill. ⇒ A surveyed lamp must win over a derived one at the same spot.
    - **⭐ SURVEYED IS NUDGED, INVENTED IS DROPPED** — *"a real recorded tree is never deleted for taste, only invented ones thin."* A surveyed item landing on illegal ground is moved to the nearest legal ground (our strip widths are soft); an invented one there is simply wrong and goes. ⇒ Exactly the policy a lamp generator needs on sidewalks, driveways and hardscape.
    - **The dissolve** — invented items thin toward the rim over the ground's fade band so the greater circle dissolves instead of ending at a seam; surveyed items are exempt. ⇒ Lamps get this free.
    ⇒ **THE DELIVERABLE IS A `derived_lamps.json` PRODUCER PLUS THE SAME PROVENANCE CONTRACT** — not a new design. ⛔ The wiring fix in H-17's head is still needed (it is how the *surveyed* well reaches the bake at all) but it is now step one of two.
