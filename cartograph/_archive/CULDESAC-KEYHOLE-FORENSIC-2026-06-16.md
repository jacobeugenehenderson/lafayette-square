- **(e) The BOOLEAN KEYHOLE (Jacob's approach), live in `tileGround`** (eye-confirmed: "the cul-de-sacs look great"). - **(f) LOCALIZED + DETECTOR.** ⛔ **(e)'s "byte-identical" was WRONG — a filter artifact.** The SV bulb shares ONE `iA` ring with a **90-vertex megatile** spanning the neighborhood; forcing that whole tile to legacy + globally closing/cleaning its curb shifted geometry **up to 473 m away** (+23 curb rings), but the byte-check bucketed the megatile (which touches the bulb) as "inside the cul-de-sac" and missed it. **FIX — the BOUNDED SPLICE:** keep the offset curb as the base, splice the closed-keyhole **only inside a disk** around each turning-circle: `blockRings = (base − disks) ∪ (keyhole ∩ disks)`, disk radius `loopR + 9 m`, disk arc from `circlePoly` (welds on both sides). **Grid-safe to 6 mm** (21 473 curb verts >30 m from a bulb deviate ≤0.0062 m from the OFF curve). Return arc tops out ~13.4°/vertex (vs the OFF 18.8° single-vertex notch). ⛔ **`clipperLib.Clipper.CleanPolygons` RETURNS a new array — it does NOT mutate in place.**

## Durable lessons (the ones worth re-reading)
1. **The proxy isn't the eye, in BOTH directions** — the render hid a 90° micro-stutter the detector caught; and the render *looked* clean while geometry shifted 473 m away. The detector + a geometric extent-check are the real gates, not the picture.
2. **Gate live (browser-reachable) code on `opts.*`, never `process.env`** — `process` is undefined in the browser; one reference crashes the whole build.
3. **Beware the filter that hides the regression** — a "byte-identical outside the cul-de-sac" check bucketed the offending megatile as "inside." Measure the *geometric* extent of a diff, not a bucketed ring-count.
4. **A cul-de-sac sharing a megatile with the grid forces locality** — any whole-tile op leaks map-wide; the bounded-disk splice is the pattern.
5. **`CleanPolygons` returns; it doesn't mutate** — check clipper-lib return semantics.
6. **Construction is the last resort** (the parent brief's §3 gate) — but here it genuinely was the curb layer, confirmed by probing, not assumed.
