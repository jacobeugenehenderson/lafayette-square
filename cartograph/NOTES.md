## 2026-06-14 (cont.) — The DERIVATION CHAIN banked; the curve-fit lands as the ONE KNOB (SSoT).

⭐ **The fundamental, recorded (Jacob: "this is a fundamental element… we MUST stop re-discovering things"):** **THE DERIVATION CHAIN** — the centerline is the ROOT source; everything is a pure derivation: **centerline → polygon (curb/tile) → ribbon**. The polygon is BOTH the geometry source AND the **identity** source — the ribbon reads *"what is a straight leg / what is a corner / treelawn-or-sidewalk"* off it — so a rough centerline corrupts *identity*, not just shape (a broken sidewalk at a faceted curve is an **identity** failure). ⇒ **fix at the centerline FIRST, at its source**; patching the polygon/ribbon on a rough centerline is editing a shadow (corollary: polygon moves but centerline doesn't ⇒ wrong layer). Banked in **`RIBBONS.md §1`** (doctrine) + **`README §START HERE`**; the concentric law (`HANDOFF-vector-curve-construction.md` Law 1) is its geometry half.

⭐ **The centerline frame→render flow, mapped (Jacob: "the whole system starts with this step"):** **`SKELETON.md §3.5`** now lays out where the centerline points live + who reads them — the **single-source** fact (`ribbons.json` points feed the navy line via `_loadCenterlines`'s `rbPoints` preference, the base line, AND the curb via `mergeLiveRibbons`), the curve-fit as a **consume-time map** (never baked into the sparse frame), the two homes, and the **IX-index constraint** (densifying stored points stales `intersections.ix` → smooth a *copy*; `segOrd`/`cornerKeyAt` are densify-robust, `ix` is not).

⭐ **The curve-fit LANDED as THE ONE KNOB (SSoT).** `smoothCenterline.js` exports **`STREET_SMOOTH`** (one tension) + **`junctionKeysOf`** (one pin-set); BOTH consumers read them — the **curb** (`buildTileGround` `opts.smooth = STREET_SMOOTH`) and the **navy** editable centerline (`MeasureOverlay` `smoothChain(…, junctionKeysOf(…))` before `polylineRibbon`) — so they are ONE smooth curve, concentric by construction; the two-source desync is now structurally impossible. Also fixed a real latent bug: THRU `splitRunAtStations` matched a smoothed-space `vi` against `streetsOrig` → threw under `smooth>0` (killed the whole tile build); now matches by junction **coordinate** (`st.at`). **Open:** tune `STREET_SMOOTH` (start `1.5`) on the eye; the **corner-aware fillet** ("part b" — `vertR=0` on within-run curve vertices so the curb doesn't fillet at every densified point); **bake-at-source** (home (b)) once the constant locks.

> **Lessons (Jacob, sharp):** (1) the navy line and the curb were two **COPIES** of `ribbons.json` points kept aligned by *discipline* (the `rbPoints` rule), **not one shared reference** — "SSoT by discipline, not by construction." That gap IS the desync I hit (smoothed the curb; the navy stayed faceted). The one knob makes it SSoT by construction. (2) **Read the docs (SKELETON) before re-deriving from code** — the frame is sparse BY DESIGN; "fix the centerline" is the consume-time curve-fit, not a skeleton edit. `[[feedback_read_canon_before_forensics]]`.
