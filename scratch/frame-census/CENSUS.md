# Frame census, 2026-11-01 night (Sigil) — BRIEF-a-frame-budget-for-every-shot §1–§4

Measured on integration 6bce89d6, the :5173 Preview, headless Chrome on this M1 (ANGLE Metal), machine load 1.3–2.6.
Per town × shot × tier: `TABLE.md` (generated by `node summarize.mjs` from `runs/*.json`). Probe: `census.mjs`.
Re-run one cell: `node scratch/frame-census/census.mjs --town=<look> --mode=desktop|phone-hi --shot=browse|hero [--close]`.

## What each column is
- **in frustum**: instanced classes per instance (bounding sphere through instanceMatrix, tree centres lifted by aGroundRaw);
  merged meshes (ground, buildings, lamps' single mesh) by the share of a vertex sample inside the frustum. FIRM.
- **unoccluded / visible≈**: a raycast from the camera to a sample of in-frustum points against the opaque ground and
  buildings only (trees and lamps do not occlude). ⚠ **UNVERIFIED at Hero**: ground-level classes read 0–16 % even after
  lifting the target 2 m and stopping the ray short. Cause not established (a ground skirt or fade mesh may be catching
  rays). Use it as a hint only; the Browse readings (≈100 %, top-down) are plausible.
- **Δ GPU ms**: the frame's GPU time with the class hidden, against shown before and after (frameCost.js#gpuWindow);
  the ± is the drift between those two. FIRM where ± is small; LS buildings and sky drift ±7–11 ms.
- **window ½**: the whole window resized to half each side (CDP device metrics). FIRM.
- Hero runs are mid-playback (the movie keeps changing the draw count), so they are not one fixed frame.

## The four findings
1. **Fill-bound everywhere on desktop.** Half the window gives 46–65 % off the frame in every town and shot (e.g. LS Hero
   228→99 ms, PTown Hero 322→176, HPDM Hero-close 133→43). This agrees with Usher's staging runs. Jacob's rule is already
   half true: a smaller window costs less. Culling now has to cut what remains: draws, geometry, and the overdraw of layers
   nobody sees.
2. **The ground is the biggest class almost everywhere**: Δ 38–102 ms on desktop (PTown Browse 102 ms, Hero 80, Hero-close
   91), with only 10–34 % of it in frustum. It is 43–52 transparent groups, each drawn whole (merged meshes, no culling) and
   stacked by renderOrder. VERDICT: **culled too little + overdraw** (stacked transparent landcover over the same pixels).
3. **Trees cost 27–74 ms at Hero** (0.85M triangles of hero impostors in LS; PTown 10.8M frame triangles), with 9–56 % of
   instances in frustum. The overhead bands are already flat (2 tris each, 17,652 in LS), so Browse trees are cheap
   (5–30 ms). VERDICT: **culled too little** (per-instance frustum culling is absent; frustumCulled false on the instanced
   meshes) **+ heavy representation past the detail cone** (the brief's three bands → one).
4. **Lamps are 2.52M triangles in every LS shot** (585 × ~4,300), drawn whether 8 % or 63 % are in frustum; Δ 8–20 ms
   desktop, 9 ms of a 25 ms phone-hi Browse frame. VERDICT: **heavy representation + culled too little** (and Grain found them
   redrawn in every shadow pass).
Buildings: Δ 15–43 ms at Hero in LS/HPDM/Huron, 45–83 % in frustum; small in PTown Browse. VERDICT: **culled too little**
(9 merged meshes, all-or-nothing). Water, displays and sky: small or within noise.

## Phone-hi
The frame is 24–27 ms at a 242×525 buffer; window ½ only takes it to 20–22 ms, so phone-hi is NOT fill-bound here. Lamps
are its biggest class (9 ms in LS Browse). Phone numbers are this desktop GPU at the phone tier's resolution, not a phone.

## The pixel-ratio question (Boz)
`renderer.setPixelRatio(×0.5)` at runtime halves the canvas but leaves EVERY render target at full size (LS: 1600×865 ×24
binds, unchanged). At CREATION, the phone-hi tier's targets match its canvas (242×525 = 242×525), because the profile's
DPR goes in as the Canvas `dpr` (qualityProfile.js) and R3F tells the composer. So it is an INSTRUMENT error (my old ×0.5
test went around R3F's store), not a product defect. The only direct setPixelRatio call in the renderer code is Arborist's
thumbnail renderer.
