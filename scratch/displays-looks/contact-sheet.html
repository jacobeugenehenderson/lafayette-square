26 looks at the real tree's proportions, with the pinnacle lit too: a strip light at the foot of each of its four faces washes the stone in the look's colour, above the cone of strands that hangs from the balcony ridge beneath it to the ground. Each look is drawn as SVG layers at the show's frame and lit here exactly as the monument would light it, one pixel per bulb. Above each card, the monument from the harbour side, motion running, at the number of colour strands chosen below; under it, the full drawing, the pinnacle's colours in the band at the top. 11 transitions and the opening Transformation. Each card plays a pair of looks: the first look, the transition, the second, and the same transition back. Transitions marked sparkle accent glint at their moving edge, the dynamic moment: random bulbs, each on its own beat, never a hard line and never the whole tree. They are kit code: the operator picks them in the show strip, never draws them.

Motions, one per layer: still · fall · rise · spin (round the tree) · spiral (round and up) · twinkle · pulse · strobe · fade (washing in over the layer beneath and back out), and three that move glyphs in place, drawn by the system: turn (the peppermints), swing (the bells), tumble (the snowflakes), build (the ribbon's lines wrapping on into a lattice, one by one). "One colour" means the motion touches only that colour.
