# photos-trade — NEW classes only

Scope: 111 of the ~116 services/shopping/uncategorised Huron listings with a website and no photo.
Already-reported classes (relative "open today", by-appointment, booking platforms, per-practitioner
schedules, wrong-entity URLs, soft 404s, rename-by-redirect, stock-on-own-CDN, alt-text-as-evidence,
home-based sole proprietors, host-owned hours) are **not** repeated here except where measurement
sharpened them into a check.

---

## 1. ⭐⭐ A CORRECT-LOOKING ALT ON A CHAIN-WIDE STOCK PHOTO — and the two-fetch check that kills it

**Dollar Tree, huro-lst-0238.** The Huron store page (`locations.dollartree.com/oh/huron/593-cleveland-rd`,
title *"Dollar Tree Store at 593 Cleveland Rd in Huron, OH"*, 11 mentions of Huron) publishes an
1800×1200 image with `alt="Dollar Tree Store Photo"` and `itemprop="image"` — the schema.org image
of that location.

**Measured:** the identical CDN id `uSIoo_xaBcR5L8MrLuEUrm-UDiTQ5tbT1Neb5MQzuJI` is served on
`/oh/sandusky/1420-sycamore-line` and `/oh/sandusky/716-crossings-rd`. One brand photograph, three
towns, each page captioning it as that store's photo.

Every existing test passes: right store's page, descriptive alt, structured-data image, under the
store heading. **Subject evidence cannot catch this, because the evidence is genuine and the picture
is not.**

▶ **THE CHECK FOR TOWN #2 — two fetches, no judgement.** On any Yext-backed chain locator
(`dyn.mktgcdn.com`), fetch a second store's page **in a different town** and diff the image ids.
Shared id ⇒ brand art. Unique id ⇒ a real location photo. Generalises to every chain locator, not
to Dollar Tree.

## 2. ⭐⭐ THE PLATFORM WRITES THE ALT — so alt text is not always the operator speaking

Two instances, opposite directions, both defeating alt-as-evidence:

- **GlossGenius (huro-lst-0134, Corner Cuts).** `static.glossgenius.com/public/**stockimage**/10/image/scissors.jpg`
  — platform stock, shared by every GlossGenius salon — carries
  `alt="Women's Haircut at Corner Cuts in Huron, OH"`. The alt names the service, the business **and
  the town**. It is a stock picture of scissors.
- **Duda (huro-lst-0247, Custom Concrete).** The alt reads *"A house with a large patio in front of
  it"* — a vision model's caption, auto-generated by the builder. Descriptive, plausible, and nobody
  at the business wrote it or checked it.

▶ **THE PATH IS TRUSTWORTHY WHERE THE ALT IS NOT.** Several platforms publish a machine-readable
own-vs-platform split, found this pass:

| platform | operator's own | platform / stock |
|---|---|---|
| GoDaddy | `img1.wsimg.com/isteam/**ip**/<site-uuid>/…` | `img1.wsimg.com/isteam/**stock**/<id>` |
| GlossGenius | `/public/**salon**/<id>/website_cover_image/…` | `/public/**stockimage**/<n>/image/<noun>.jpg` |
| Showit | `static.showit.co/<size>/<hash>/**183666**/…` (account id) | `…/<hash>/**shared**/…` |
| Square Appointments | `**seller-brand-assets**-f.squarecdn.com/<seller>/…` | `square-web-production-f`, `appointments-production-f` |
| Booksy | `/biz_photo/`, `/inspiration/`, `/service_photos/` | `/review_photos/` = **the customer's**, different rights holder |

Three Huron records (0099 Alicia Wolph Roshong, 0110 Mark Myers DDS, 0165 RB's Services) are
**100% `/isteam/stock/`** — the path alone settles them, no filename heuristic needed.

## 3. ⭐ A VENDOR THAT SHIPS ITS OWN PLACEHOLDER TEST

**Edward Jones (0105, 0090).** Every advisor portrait is
`<img src="…/fa-pictures/600x900/1668022.jpg" data-default-img="…/fa-pictures/default/photo.jpg">`.
`src == data-default-img` ⇒ no portrait; `!=` ⇒ real one. **The page states the discriminator.**
Worth grepping for on arrival in a new town: some vendors hand you the check.

## 4. ⭐⭐ A DEAD CHAIN STORE ID THAT LANDS YOU IN ANOTHER STATE

**U-Haul, huro-lst-0285.** `uhaul.com/Locations/Truck-Rentals-near-Huron-OH-44839/048773/` follows
redirects to `uhaul.com/Locations/**Danville-IL-61832**/Results/`. HTTP 200, 248 KB of healthy
content, **zero occurrences of "Huron"**.

This beats both existing tests: liveness passes, and the soft-404 md5-against-the-root test passes
too because the landing page is *not* the home page — it is a real page for a real other place.
▶ **The only cheap catch is `grep -ci <town>` on the LANDED body, plus reading `%{url_effective}`.**
Make town-count part of every fetch, not a follow-up.

## 5. ⛔⛔ A BANK'S DOMAIN NOW 301s TO AN OFFSHORE GAMBLING SITE

**Civista Bank, huro-lst-0151.** `http://www.citizensbankco.com` → `301 Moved Permanently` →
`https://joker123.co.uk/`. ("Citizens Bank Co." is Civista's former name; the lapsed domain was
resold.) The riverviewlanes.com pattern, but on a **bank**.

⭐ **The new part: the destination then answers 520/522 with a 16-byte body**, so a checker that only
records the final status logs *"site down, retry later"* and never sees where it pointed. **Log the
final HOST on every redirect chain, not only the final status** — a failing destination is exactly
when the redirect target matters most.

## 6. ⭐ AN ENTIRE SHARED MICROSITE HOST LAPSED, TAKING EVERY TENANT AT ONCE

`*.placeweb.site` now serves a **Sedo domain-parking** page. Two of our records live there
(0174 Angles Hair Design, 0123 Bores Cycle) and both are gone in one stroke. Not a per-business
failure — a **hosting-suffix** failure.
▶ Group held URLs by registrable domain **before** fetching; one dead shared host explains N records
and costs one request. Same logic would have grouped the four `exxon.com` 403s and the three
`bluerhino.com` finder links.

## 7. ⭐ THE 200-OK DEAD ENDS THIS TOWN INVENTED, each needing its own detector

All of these return HTTP 200 or a non-obvious status and would read as "site OK":

- **JS-onload parking lander** (0002 **Sawmill Creek**, 0148 debcsellsohio) — 114 bytes,
  `<script>window.onload=function(){location.href="/lander"}</script>`. **No HTTP redirect at all**,
  so curl reports 200 at the original URL and follows nothing. ⭐ Tell: body under ~200 bytes.
- **HTML 4.01 `<frameset>` as the whole site** (0208 JasonSmithListings.com) — 583 bytes wrapping a
  live, rich page at `ohio.simplybetterrealty.com/jason-smith/`. Reads as an empty site; isn't.
- **Vercel `DEPLOYMENT_NOT_FOUND`** (0154 Gordon Lumber) — HTTP 404 with a 107-byte
  **`text/plain`** body. An HTML-shaped scraper gets nothing *and no error*.
- **Wix "ConnectYourDomain Error"** (0140 harmonicegghuron.com) — 200, 2.5 KB of platform error.
- **Apache directory index, "Index of /"** (0261 Reiki and Counseling Center) — 200, empty listable web root.
- **HugeDomains "<name>.com is for sale"** (0163 Elw Enterprises) — 200, 43 KB, fifteen images, all reseller chrome.
- **eBay HTTP 410 Gone** (0130 Firwood Collectibles) — a *definitive* answer, unlike a 404. Honour it.
- **DNS SERVFAIL ≠ NXDOMAIN** (0164 Huron Recovery Specialists vs 0079 Laguna Yacht Club / 0003 Erie
  Supply House / 0124 Marcella's / 0147 Shimer / 0199 Wray). SERVFAIL = registered, delegation broken.
  NXDOMAIN = gone. A checker that records "fetch failed" for both loses which one is recoverable.
- **`www.` is not free** (0170 Custom Marine): `www.custommarine.biz` does not resolve, `custommarine.biz` does.

## 8. ⭐ A LIVE PAGE WITH A DEAD IMAGE LAYER

**North Coast Propeller Repair, huro-lst-0133.** 38 KB of healthy HTML with 21 `<img>` tags and the
best alt text on any site in the slice (*"Propeller Repair and Truing"*, *"PROPSCAN Services —
Authorized Facility"*, *"Come see us at the Catawba Island Boat Show"*). **Every image URL 404s** —
slider, content blocks, category tiles, social icons, and the site's own logo.

This is the inverse of every other failure here: the *labels* are perfect and the *pixels* are gone.
▶ A photo pass that records URLs from markup without a HEAD would have shipped 21 broken images with
excellent captions. **HEAD every candidate.** (It also means the operator does not know their site
renders text-only.)

## 9. ⭐ "JS-only, therefore empty" — the score on this slice

The brief says it is usually false. Confirmed, with one honest exception:

- **Square Online** (0267 Ruhe Haus) — page model in the HTML, uploads visible as escaped
  `\/uploads\/b\/…`. Bennett's lesson holds.
- **Wix** (0092 Mesenburg, 0141 Lakefront, 0181 Blue Ribbon) — warmup JSON carries a **`fileName`**
  field that survives even when the rendered `alt` is empty. On Mesenburg every alt is `""` and the
  filenames (`SNP_5446.jpg`, `marketing 22-104_edited.jpg`) are the *only* subject evidence; they are
  only in the JSON.
- **Squarespace** (0217, 0249, 0027, 0037) — plain markup; `?format=json-pretty` works but returned
  an empty `items` array on 0027 Label Aid, whose photo was on an interior page instead.
- **Lazy-load `data-src`** (0240 Lisa's Paws, 0242 Ace Hardware, 0017 River Monster) — `src` is a
  base64 SVG or 1×1 gif placeholder. An `img/@src` pass sees **zero images on sites that have a dozen**.
- ⛔ **EXCEPTION — Vagaro** (0155 Integrative Massotherapy): 246 KB of HTML containing only Vagaro's
  own chrome (logo, four country flags, a HIPAA badge). **No data channel.** Unlike Booksy and
  GlossGenius, Vagaro publishes nothing fetchable. This is the one place the claim held.

## 10. ⭐ THE FRONT DOOR IS NOT THE SITE

**Label Aid (0027):** the home page has **no images at all** and `?format=json-pretty` returns zero
items — yet `/company` carries the best alt text found anywhere in this slice:
*"The front entrance of Label Aid, showing our modern building with an all glass wall, located at
**608 Rye Beach Road, Huron, OH 44839**"* — it states the street address, which is a self-verifying
identity check.
**River Monster Tours (0017):** home page yields one hero; the gallery lives on `/tour/<name>/` pages.
**RS Cycle (0009):** home page has category tiles; the only real photo is on `/about/`.
▶ **A zero on the home page is not a zero.** Always follow `/about`, `/company`, `/gallery` and the
product/tour pages before recording "no photos".

## 11. ⭐ NEW CONFESSING FILENAMES (extends stock-on-own-CDN)

Beyond `unsplash-image-*` / `FB_IMG_*` / `Screenshot*` / Canva:

- **`pexels-<contributor>-<id>.jpeg`** (0113) · **`AdobeStock_<id>`** (0186, 0194) · **`iStock-<id>`** (0009)
- **`<photo-id>_<secret>_z.jpg`** — a **Flickr** download filename (0181 Blue Ribbon:
  `34449104766_e436575f6a_z (1).jpg`). Looks like noise; is a provenance trail.
- **`<numeric>_<numeric>_<numeric>_n.jpg`** — the **bare Facebook CDN** name, *not* the `FB_IMG_`
  form we look for (0247 Custom Concrete, 0009 RS Cycle). Usually the operator's **own** Facebook
  photos, so this one is a *good* sign — but you must recognise it to score it.
- **`<product-code>_700x700__<id>.<epoch>.1280.1280.webp`** — supplier catalogue asset (0009).
- **Wix media prefix `11062b`** is **Wix's own stock/demo account** — confirmed on two unrelated Huron
  sites (0141 "Sunset", 0181 "Buffet Table Display"). ⚠️ But the prefix rule is a *question*, not a
  verdict: on 0092 Mesenburg the foreign prefix `c2111b` carries `Smoked Wings.jpg` and `IMG_5508.jpeg`,
  which read as real food, not theme art.
- **`-Picsart`** (0017) — app-composited, not a photograph.

## 12. ⭐⭐ AI-GENERATED IMAGERY IS NOW A PHOTO-PASS CLASS OF ITS OWN

Three instances in one slice, all self-confessing in the filename:

- `ChatGPT+Image+Jan+9,+2026,+02_03_02+PM.png` — **Huron Market's `og:image`** (0249). Their social
  card is not a photo of the store.
- `ChatGPTImageJul26_2026_05_52_55PM.png` ×4 — CrystalMic's product shots (0030).
- `Jason_Smith_Gemini_Generated_Image.png` — **a realtor's portrait** (0208).

This is not the same problem as stock. Stock is a real photograph of the wrong place; **this is not a
photograph**, and on a portrait it depicts a person who does not exist as shown.
▶ Grep candidates for `ChatGPT`, `Gemini_Generated`, `_ai_`, `dall-e`, `midjourney`, `firefly`.

## 13. ⭐ DROPSHIP AND STAGING LEAKAGE IN THE ALT AND THE HOST

- **CrystalMic (0030):** `alt="alishop:c4323ea7fbbe"` — an AliExpress→Shopify importer's internal
  token left in the alt. The alt field itself proves the image came in with a supplier feed.
- **Diva Lashes (0043):** a link thumbnail named `81lk10kQbvL.-CR0-0-1290-1290-.-US500-SCLZZZZZZZ-.jpeg`
  — an **Amazon** product-image id, saved and re-uploaded.
- **Central Basin Bait / Tattered Wings (0088):** a live page hotlinks an image from
  `box2513.temp.domains/~aofbpvmy/**gobblercompany**/wp-content/…` — a cPanel **temporary-domain
  staging install of a third, unrelated brand**. A production page serving out of somebody's sandbox.

## 14. ⭐ WRONG-ENTITY WHERE EVERY TOWN TEST PASSES

The known class said: confirm the page states this town, this street or this phone. **Huron Family
Dental Center (0012) defeats that.** Its held URL `huronhs.com` is **Huron City Schools** —
419-433-1234, the stadium, the superintendent, *"Let's Talk Huron at Gathering Grounds"*. Huron is
everywhere on the page. `huronhs` = Huron **High School**, not Huron Health.
▶ **Town identity is necessary and nowhere near sufficient.** The test must be *this business* — name
tokens or the exact held phone — not *this town*. (Cf. 0045 Huron Lake Front Market → the Greater
Sandusky Partnership, a chamber; 0088 → a waterfowl outfitter in another exchange.)
