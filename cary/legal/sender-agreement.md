## 1. Purpose
Lafayette Square Deliveries is a neighborhood-based delivery-coordination service connecting Senders, independent couriers, and customers within the service area `‹rider›`. The Platform is a digital marketplace that facilitates order transmission and payment among participating Senders, couriers, and customers. The Platform does not prepare food, sell goods, or perform deliveries; it provides technology services only and does not control the manner or method of Sender operations or courier transportation. **The Sender remains the merchant of record for all sales.**

## 2. Sender Types
This Agreement applies to all Senders; the following type-specific rules attach where indicated:

| Sender type | Key attributes | Alcohol pathway (§9) |
|---|---|---|
| **Restaurant** (on-premises, by-the-drink licensee) | prepares food on-premises; merchant of record | meal-attached to-go only (RSMo 311.202) |
| **Package store** (off-premises retail liquor licensee) | sells sealed packaged alcohol as core business | no meal requirement |
| **General merchant** (future — sealed retail goods) | sells/ships goods; merchant of record | n/a unless licensed for alcohol |
| **Peer sender** (future — non-sale parcel) | not a sale; sends a parcel | n/a |

> **▸ Rationale (alcohol branches by type).** The meal-attachment rule is *specific to restaurants/bars*, not universal — a package liquor store has no meal requirement (its whole business is sealed off-premises alcohol). Branching alcohol by Sender type captures both correctly. A **peer sender** (neighbor sending a parcel, no sale) may fall outside marketplace-facilitator sales-tax entirely but picks up parcel-handling liability — to be covered when that type is enabled.

## 3. Sender Status & Responsibilities
The Sender is solely responsible for: preparation/sourcing of the goods, food safety (where applicable), accuracy of listings and pricing, packaging, compliance with health and licensing regulations, and (where alcohol is offered) all alcohol licensing and compliance.

## 4. Service Area
Delivery is limited to the service area: **Chouteau Avenue to Interstate 44, Jefferson Avenue to Truman Parkway, including adjacent buildings** `‹rider: service area›`. Availability depends on courier participation and Sender hours.

## 5. Ordering and Payment
Customers place orders through the Platform. At checkout, customers pay: the subtotal; applicable sales tax; a payment-processing fee, if applicable; and a service charge `‹rider: fee schedule›`. **The Sender receives 100% of the subtotal and applicable sales tax. The Platform deducts no commission from Sender sales.**

### 5.1 Minimum Order
A minimum order applies — currently **$40 before tax and fees** `‹rider›` — to ensure fair courier compensation.

### 5.2 Payment Processing and Limited Agency
The Platform acts as the Sender's limited payment agent solely to collect customer payments at checkout and distribute funds to the Sender and courier as described here. Customer payments collected through the Platform are payments made directly to the Sender for amounts owed to the Sender. Processing is performed by a third-party processor; any disclosed processing fee is charged to the customer and used solely to cover processing costs.

### 5.3 Sales Tax & Marketplace-Facilitator Status `‹rider›`
The Sender, as merchant of record, is responsible for the sales tax on its sales. **Open item — near-term, not distant.** Under Missouri's marketplace-facilitator law (RSMo 144.752), once the Platform crosses economic nexus it may itself be statutorily obligated to collect and remit Missouri tax on the facilitated sales, and a contract cannot override a statute.

**What the threshold actually measures.** Economic nexus is **$100,000 in gross receipts from the *facilitated taxable sales*** — i.e., the **food subtotals flowing through the Platform** (what customers pay Senders for food), measured **gross**, over a rolling 12 months, assessed each quarter-end, with no transaction-count component. It is **NOT** the Platform's revenue, **NOT** the service charge, **NOT** the Platform's 25% share, and **NOT** profit. Because it is measured on the *total food sales* and not the Platform's ~5.5% slice (see breakdown below), it is reached far sooner than a "revenue" reading suggests — plausibly **within the first months of operation** at neighborhood order values.

> **The slice, for orientation.** Platform take = 25% of the 22% service charge = **5.5% of the food subtotal**; courier = 16.5%. So $100k of facilitated food sales = $22k of service charge → ~$5,500 to the Platform. The facilitator obligation can attach by the time the *Platform itself* has earned only ~$5,500.

The allocation of who remits, on this zero-commission, not-merchant-of-record model, is **flagged for an EARLY (pre- or near-launch) Missouri DOR letter ruling** — see readiness brief.

> **▸ Rationale (facilitator tax).** The "no commission" structure does **not** exempt the Platform — the statute keys on transmitting "all *or part*" of the payment, not on taking a cut — and the threshold is the *gross facilitated food sales*, not the Platform's fee, so it arrives early. This is operational machinery (DOR registration, local-rate collection, remittance) to design near launch, not "before scaling." Resolve who-remits by letter ruling first.

## 6. Service-Charge Distribution
The service charge supports delivery operations and is separate from the Sender's sale. As of the effective date: the service charge is **22% of the subtotal** `‹rider›`; of that, **75% goes to the courier** and **25% is retained by the Platform**. **Customer tips, if any, belong entirely to the courier.** The Platform may modify the fee schedule (percentage, split, minimum) on notice to the Sender before changes take effect.

## 7. Listing Control — the Guardian
Senders manage their own menus/catalog, prices, specials, and availability through a designated **Guardian** account — a trusted manager or representative authorized to maintain the listing (see `places-guardian-terms.md`). The Sender is responsible for the accuracy of all listings, prices, and descriptions.

## 8. Availability & Order Preparation
Senders control their own availability; orders are offered to customers only when the Sender is accepting orders and couriers are available. Senders prepare orders promptly, package appropriately for transport, clearly label for pickup, and provide orders to couriers in a condition suitable for transport.

## 9. Alcohol Orders
Where alcohol is offered, the **Sender is the licensed seller** and is responsible for all alcohol compliance; **the Platform holds no liquor license** and is delivery-only. The Courier must be **21+** and must verify ID at the door (Courier Agreement §8).

**Restaurant senders** (RSMo 311.202 `‹rider›`): alcohol to-go/delivery is permitted **only** when —
- the alcohol accompanies a meal prepared on-premises, **ordered simultaneously**;
- the number of sealed drinks does **not exceed roughly twice the number of meals** ordered `‹rider — confirm exact ratio + volume cap with ATC›`;
- each container is **filled and sealed by the Sender's 21+ staff** in a tamperproof, transparent, securely-sealed bag or with tamperproof tape (rigid/leakproof; total-volume cap applies); and
- the customer is 21+ (verified at the door).

The Platform's checkout enforces the meal-to-drink ratio and blocks non-conforming alcohol orders.

**Package-store senders:** sealed packaged alcohol for off-premises consumption may be sold and delivered **without** a meal requirement, subject to the 21+ courier, door-side ID check, and the store's own licensing.

> **▸ Rationale (meal-ratio in the UI).** Encoding `drinks ≤ ~2× meals` as a hard checkout constraint protects the restaurant's license automatically, rather than relying on staff to catch it. Exact ratio + ounce cap are a confirm-with-ATC number.

## 10. Refunds and Errors
Allocation: the **Sender** is responsible for incorrect orders, missing items, and food-quality issues; the **Courier** for delivery mishandling. The Platform may issue refunds at its discretion to preserve customer goodwill. Before deducting any refund from future Sender payouts, the Platform gives notice describing the basis; the Sender may dispute within five (5) business days.

## 11. Privacy
The Platform is a privacy-respecting neighborhood service and does not retain personal customer data beyond what is temporarily required to complete transactions. Payment data is handled by the payment processor.

## 12. Marketing
Senders may promote their participation; the Platform may feature participating Senders within the service.

## 13. Termination
Either party may discontinue participation at any time. The Platform may modify features, policies, or procedures, and may suspend or remove participants to protect the integrity, safety, or lawful operation of the service.

## 14. Liability
The Platform provides order-coordination and technology services only and is not responsible for preparation, food safety, Sender compliance, listing/order accuracy, or courier transportation. The Platform does not operate as a restaurant, delivery service, or employer of participating couriers.

## 15. Indemnification
The Sender agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Platform and its owners, officers, employees, and agents from claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: the preparation, packaging, sale, or service of goods by the Sender; food-safety or foodborne-illness claims; alcohol sales or compliance; inaccurate listings or pricing; violations of health or other laws; or the Sender's breach of this Agreement. This applies to claims by customers, couriers, regulators, or third parties. The indemnifying party agrees to cooperate reasonably in defense.

## 16. Dispute Resolution
Informal resolution first; failing that, either party may bring a claim in a court of competent jurisdiction in the State of Missouri `‹rider›`, including small-claims court where applicable.
