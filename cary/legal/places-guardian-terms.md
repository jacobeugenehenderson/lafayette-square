# Places & Guardian Terms

The terms governing **Places** (the maintained profiles for neighborhood locations) and **Guardians** (the people who maintain them) — the MySpace-style profile layer of the Platform.

## 1. What a Place is
A **Place** is a profile for a neighborhood location (a restaurant, shop, residence, park, landmark, or other point on the map). Place content — descriptions, photos, history, menus, hours, and other details — is contributed and maintained by **Guardians**, not authored by the Platform.

> **▸ Rationale (the architecture).** The discovery/identity layer is built as **profiles maintained by their Guardians**, not as a database the Platform scrapes or aggregates from third parties. This is deliberate: it sidesteps any copying of proprietary listing data, and it means contributed content reaches the Platform under a license the Guardian grants (§4), not a scrape the Platform performs.

## 2. What a Guardian is
A **Guardian** is a person authorized to create and maintain a Place's profile — for a business, a trusted manager or representative the business designates; for other Places, a contributor the Platform recognizes. A Place may have one or more Guardians. The Platform may verify a Guardian's authority over a Place before granting or continuing access.

## 3. Guardian Responsibilities
Guardians agree to:
- maintain accurate, current content for their Place;
- post only content they have the right to post (their own, or content they are licensed/authorized to use);
- not post unlawful, infringing, deceptive, or harmful content;
- not impersonate a Place or a person, or claim a Place they have no authority over.

The Guardian — not the Platform — is responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of content they post.

## 4. Content License
By posting content to a Place, the Guardian grants the Platform a **non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to host, display, reproduce, and adapt that content** for the purpose of operating, presenting, and promoting the service. The Guardian retains ownership of their content. The license continues for content that remains posted and for reasonable operational backups after removal; it does not transfer ownership.

> **▸ Rationale (why the license is required).** Without an explicit display license, the Platform has no clear right to show Guardian-contributed photos, history, and descriptions. This grant is the clean, contributor-sourced alternative to scraping — and it is the legal seam that lets the "people add their own detail to the buildings" vision operate.

## 5. Platform Role — Interactive Service, Not Publisher (Section 230)
The Platform hosts content created by Guardians and other users. As an interactive computer service, the Platform is **not the publisher or speaker** of Guardian- or user-contributed content and is not responsible for it (47 U.S.C. § 230). The Platform may, but is not obligated to, review, moderate, edit, or remove content, and doing so does not make it the author. Content reflects its Guardians, not the Platform.

> **▸ Rationale (§230 posture).** Stating the interactive-service posture preserves the Platform's federal protection from liability for third-party content while leaving it free to moderate. Both halves matter: the shield, and the freedom to take down bad content without becoming its "publisher."

## 6. Moderation & Takedown
The Platform may remove content or suspend a Guardian or Place to protect the safety, integrity, or lawful operation of the service, or in response to a valid legal request or rights-holder complaint. Where practical, the Platform acts relationally and with notice — consistent with a neighborhood service — except where immediate action is required.

## 7. Privacy
Place and Guardian content is presented publicly by nature. The Platform minimizes collection of personal data beyond what operating the profiles and the service requires. Guardians should not post others' private information without authorization.

## 8. Relationship to Other Agreements
For a Sender, the Guardian maintains the Sender's listing under the Sender Participation Agreement; these Terms govern the *content/profile* layer, that Agreement governs *participation in deliveries*. A conflict between the two is resolved in favor of the more specific provision for the matter at issue.

## 9. Changes & Acceptance
These Terms become effective when a Guardian creates or maintains a Place or accepts them through onboarding. The Platform may modify them by notice through the Platform or by email; unless required sooner for safety or law, changes take effect no earlier than thirty (30) days after notice.
